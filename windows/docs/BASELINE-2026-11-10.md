# Offscreen baseline, 2026-11-10

This is diagnostic evidence for #26 and an opportunity list for #35, not a
public-release performance acceptance result. The occupied workstation was not
reserved or isolated from background activity. Local repository validation also
overlapped part of the initial idle/search controls. No real presentation,
installed profile, injected input, clipboard write, system-wide trace or native
tiling was used.

## Initial cold-cache measurements

Values are means of side A in A/A. CPU is total CPU through process exit,
not steady-state idle utilization. Work is a synthetic per-step span.

| Scenario | Ready, ms | CPU, ms | Peak private commit, MiB | Work, ms/step | A/A CPU verdict |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | --- |
| Idle drawer graph | 629.3 | 510.4 | 67.40 | 0, below timer resolution | Inconclusive |
| Search, 100 queries/step | 655.8 | 10343.8 | 75.35 | 1239.61 | Inconclusive |
| Panels including Nexus | 628.8 | 921.9 | 103.15 | Not a panel-latency measurement | Inconclusive |
| Synthetic audio/media | 639.9 | 494.8 | 68.39 | 0.292 | Inconclusive |

The panel work span in this initial suite excludes its open/close dispatch.
Its zero value must not be used as evidence of fast panel animation.
Eight steps per trial do not support p95/p99 claims.

## Controls and limits

The added-cost control requested a 75-ms test-only CPU spin per step, nominally
600 ms per trial. The fixed diagnostic floors were 25 ms CPU and 10 ms work.
The following intervals describe actual paired B minus A differences.

| Scenario | CPU added cost, ms, 95% interval | Work added cost, ms/step, 95% interval | Qualification |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Idle | 565.1, 527.8 to 602.4 | 75.0, 75.0 to 75.0 | Host timer plant detected; CPU A/A inconclusive |
| Search | 751.3, 574.4 to 928.2 | 77.76, 66.59 to 88.93 | Both plants detected; both A/A metrics inconclusive |
| Panels | 536.5, 465.5 to 607.4 | 75.0, 75.0 to 75.0 | Host timer plant detected; not panel latency |

Initial A/A CPU interval half-widths were 25.7 ms idle, 154.2 ms search,
92.2 ms panels and 34.5 ms audio/media. The search work half-width was
12.83 ms, wider than its 10-ms floor. No CPU metric qualified at that floor.
A detected large plant does not repair an inconclusive A/A.

Readiness, private memory, animation/frame tails, native callbacks, GPU,
capture and long-session retention remain unqualified. Private memory's
A/A `not-detected` result is not an added-memory control or equivalence.
The initial integer-millisecond timer cannot establish sub-millisecond
sensitivity, even when its reported interval is zero.
