public-release performance acceptance result. The occupied workstation was not
reserved or isolated from background activity. Local repository validation also
overlapped part of the initial idle/search controls. No real presentation,
installed profile, injected input, clipboard write, system-wide trace or native
tiling was used.

## Controls and limits

The added-cost control requested a 75-ms test-only CPU spin per step, nominally
600 ms per trial. The fixed diagnostic floors were 25 ms CPU and 10 ms work.
The following intervals describe actual paired B minus A differences.

| Scenario | CPU added cost, ms, 95% interval | Work added cost, ms/step, 95% interval | Qualification |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Idle | 565.1, 527.8 to 602.4 | 75.0, 75.0 to 75.0 | Host timer plant detected; CPU A/A inconclusive |
| Search | 751.3, 574.4 to 928.2 | 77.76, 66.59 to 88.93 | Both plants detected; both A/A metrics inconclusive |
| Panels | 536.5, 465.5 to 607.4 | 75.0, 75.0 to 75.0 | Host timer plant detected; not panel latency |

Initial A/A CPU interval half-widths were 25.7 ms idle, 154.2 ms search,
92.2 ms panels and 34.5 ms audio/media. The search work half-width was
12.83 ms, wider than its 10-ms floor. No CPU metric qualified at that floor.
A detected large plant does not repair an inconclusive A/A.

Readiness, private memory, animation/frame tails, native callbacks, GPU,
capture and long-session retention remain unqualified. Private memory's
A/A `not-detected` result is not an added-memory control or equivalence.
The initial integer-millisecond timer cannot establish sub-millisecond
sensitivity, even when its reported interval is zero.

Base resource-polling/marker overhead relative to the public release remains
not measured. Profiling is unsuitable for confirmation memory totals here.

## Ranked trace-backed opportunities for #35

The successful panel profile was 23,893,099 bytes with 9,320 duration groups.
Trace SHA256 was
`571b3504783d44de8cfd3fc8f195077d90449e8254b592a50b39efba372837d1`.
Qt supplies QML source locations, not native CPU-stack attribution. Times below
are inclusive and overlap. They are not additive or whole-app CPU fractions.
The test host's own 344.31-ms compile range is excluded from the app ranking.

1. Cold launcher dependency compilation. The staged Actions module had one
   219.84-ms compile range, and ContentWindow had one 111.75-ms compile range.
   Inspect import-time work in
   `windows/shell/adapters/modules/launcher/services/Actions.qml` and its
   dependencies before changing search algorithms. Proposed target is at least
   50 ms and 20% off the identified cold range, plus a separately confirmed
   readiness benefit of at least 50 ms and 8%. Qualify a readiness added-delay
   control first. Frequency here is once per cold process. Deferring actions
   risks changing first-use availability, so preserve action/cancellation tests.

2. Nexus page registry construction. `PageCompRegistry.qml` had one 100.23-ms
   compile range. Inspect eager page imports in
   `windows/shell/adapters/modules/nexus/PageCompRegistry.qml`.
   Proposed target is at least 25 ms and 25% off this identified cold range.
   Establish a focused cold Nexus-open metric before implementation.
   A cold compile event does not prove a warm reopen cost. Preserve page
   loading, reuse, close and teardown behavior.

3. Dashboard creation on opening. The Windows dashboard wrapper had six
   binding ranges totaling 74.34 ms at its loader binding. The dashboard
   content had four Repeater creation ranges totaling 64.46 ms and sixteen
   Loader creation ranges totaling 63.79 ms. These nested ranges describe
   overlapping work. Inspect
   `windows/shell/adapters/modules/dashboard/Wrapper.qml` and
   `modules/dashboard/Content.qml`. Proposed target is at least 15 ms and
   20% in a focused dashboard-open metric. Qualify that metric below the
   target first. Any retention/reuse experiment needs a separate cooldown
   memory check and must preserve original animation and ownership.

Search remains an unranked follow-up. Its large synthetic cost warrants a
bounded focused profile, but the full-feature search profile hit the 20-second
trial deadline. No intact search profile or CPU self-time claim is available.
Reduce diagnostic event volume or use a smaller declared profiling workload,
then confirm against the original uninstrumented workload.

These are hypotheses and proposed targets, not implemented savings.
Whole-app fractions and real interaction frequency are unknown. Stop any
experiment after three inconclusive bounded confirmations, a behavior
regression, or failure to clear the independently qualified absolute target.
Do not optimize an instrumented inclusive range alone.
